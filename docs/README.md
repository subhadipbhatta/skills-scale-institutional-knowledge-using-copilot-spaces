# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the central entry point for the OctoAcme project management documentation set. It gives a quick overview of how the team runs projects and links to the supporting guidance in this folder.

## Project management process summary
OctoAcme follows a lightweight, cross-functional project lifecycle designed to keep work aligned to customer value, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes.

1. Initiation: define the problem, stakeholders, success criteria, and scope to confirm whether the project should move forward.
2. Planning: break the work into prioritized backlog items, identify milestones, dependencies, and responsibilities, and align on the definition of done.
3. Execution & tracking: deliver in small increments with regular check-ins, visible progress, and clear ownership of blockers and risks.
4. Risk & communication: keep stakeholders informed, track issues and dependencies, and escalate concerns early to maintain momentum.
5. Release & deployment: validate readiness, deploy with clear checks and rollback readiness, and communicate outcomes to stakeholders.
6. Retrospective & continuous improvement: review what worked, capture lessons learned, and turn them into follow-up actions.

Across the lifecycle, OctoAcme emphasizes customer-first thinking, iterative delivery, data-informed decisions, and continuous improvement.
