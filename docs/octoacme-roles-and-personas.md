# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate day-to-day execution across teams and functions. They keep work moving, monitor progress, and surface risks before they become blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Own cross-functional scheduling and delivery cadence
- Track milestones, dependencies, and work in progress
- Coordinate daily execution with project managers, team leads, and stakeholders
- Escalate capacity or dependency issues early
- Provide regular progress updates and recovery plans

### Goals
- Maintain predictable execution flow
- Reduce delivery friction across teams
- Keep stakeholders informed on progress and risk

### Typical Communication
- Daily or weekly delivery syncs
- Milestone reviews and dependency updates
- Status reports for project sponsors and managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager on planning, risks, and reporting rhythm
- Aligns with Product Managers to confirm priority and release sequencing
- Supports Developers and Technical Leads by removing blockers and clarifying execution trade-offs

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide the solution approach, technical standards, and validation strategy. They partner with project and product stakeholders to translate requirements into feasible delivery plans.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architecture guardrails
- Validate feasibility, estimates, and implementation approach
- Guide design decisions and technical trade-off discussions
- Support testing strategy and release readiness checks
- Identify technical risks and mitigation options early

### Goals
- Deliver secure, maintainable, and scalable solutions
- Balance speed, quality, and technical sustainability
- Ensure the implementation matches the agreed product direction

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical risk briefings with project leadership
- Implementation status updates during milestone reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to shape implementation and review quality standards
- Works with Project Managers to assess effort, sequencing, and dependencies
- Advises Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and delivery impacts

---

## Customer Advocate / Product Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Customer Advocates represent end-user and business needs throughout the project lifecycle. They help ensure that delivery decisions stay connected to customer value and business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Represent user needs, feedback, and business context
- Validate requirements, acceptance criteria, and priorities
- Participate in roadmap and release decisions
- Help resolve product trade-offs and scope questions
- Confirm that delivered outcomes meet stakeholder expectations

### Goals
- Keep the team focused on customer value
- Improve product fit and adoption
- Maintain alignment between user needs and delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- Feedback loops with product, delivery, and stakeholders
- Requirements and acceptance-criteria reviews
- Customer or user-impact briefings during milestone checkpoints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define and refine priorities
- Provides input to Project Managers and Delivery Managers on sequencing and release readiness
- Helps Developers and Technical Leads understand real-world needs and success criteria

---

## Operations or Support Owner

### Role Summary
Operations or Support Owners ensure that systems, processes, and support readiness are in place before and after release. They focus on service continuity, operational readiness, and smooth handoff into live support.

### Responsibilities
- Validate deployment readiness and operational resilience
- Define monitoring, support, and incident-response expectations
- Coordinate support handoff and runbook preparation
- Identify operational dependencies and release risks
- Participate in readiness reviews before go-live

### Goals
- Reduce user disruption and support burden after release
- Ensure a clear service ownership model
- Keep operational risk visible and managed

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and operational checklists
- Support transition meetings and incident planning
- Operational status updates during launch and stabilization

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers and Delivery Managers on release timing and readiness gates
- Coordinates with Technical Leads and Developers on monitoring, rollback, and support requirements
- Supports Product Managers by confirming that service expectations match customer commitments

---

## Change Coordinator / Communication Lead

### Role Summary
Change Coordinators manage stakeholder communication, release impacts, and adoption planning. They keep people informed so planned changes are understood and supported across the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate communication plans for milestones and releases
- Clarify change impacts, adoption steps, and support expectations
- Align messaging with project sponsors, delivery leads, and stakeholders
- Support stakeholder readiness and issue escalation communication
- Maintain a clear narrative of what is changing and why

### Goals
- Increase stakeholder confidence and adoption
- Minimize confusion during transitions
- Ensure communication is timely, consistent, and role-appropriate

### Typical Communication
- Release notes, stakeholder briefs, and email updates
- Change-impact summaries and readiness communications
- Cross-functional coordination meetings and status updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to synchronize plans and updates
- Aligns with Product Managers to ensure communication reflects business outcomes and user expectations
- Supports Operations Owners during launch and stabilization by reinforcing support guidance

---

## Security / Compliance Reviewer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Reviewers evaluate policies, controls, and risks across the project. They help ensure the solution meets organizational standards and reduces avoidable risk before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements, technical designs, and implementation choices for risk
- Validate compliance with policy, privacy, and access requirements
- Identify remediation steps and control gaps
- Provide guidance for secure deployment and support practices
- Participate in risk review and decision points

### Goals
- Prevent avoidable security and compliance issues
- Make risk decisions early and transparently
- Ensure controls are built in rather than bolted on

### Typical Communication
- Risk and compliance reviews
- Design and control checkpoints
- Security sign-offs and escalation notes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Technical Leads and Developers to identify control requirements early
- Works with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to protect schedule integrity and readiness gates
- Supports Product Managers and Customer Advocates by highlighting business and user risk considerations

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intended to work together: Product and Customer perspectives define direction, Project and Delivery roles maintain execution, Technical and Operations roles enable implementation and readiness, and Security and Communication roles reduce risk and improve adoption.

---

## How these personas interact with existing roles
- Product Managers and Customer Advocates define the value and success criteria.
- Project Managers and Delivery Managers coordinate scope, schedule, dependencies, and reporting.
- Developers and Technical Leads translate priorities into buildable, testable, and maintainable solutions.
- Operations or Support Owners and Change Coordinators prepare the team and stakeholders for launch and adoption.
- Security and Compliance Reviewers validate risk decisions and enforce controls across the lifecycle.
- Together these roles create a clearer model of ownership, decision rights, handoffs, and escalation paths across the project lifecycle.
